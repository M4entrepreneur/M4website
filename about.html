ABOUT THE M4 ENTREPRENEUR

I'm Joe Molineaux, The M4 Entrepreneur. I'm an entrepreneurship educator, business strategist and consultant who helps entrepreneurs and business owners make better decisions and move their ideas and business forward.

Most entrepreneurs aren't short on information. In a world of AI, what's hard is knowing which information matters, which opportunities are worth pursuing, and which actions move the business forward. That's why I built The M4 Method™, a practical way to strengthen the four areas every business runs on: Management, Marketing, Money and Media. Here you'll find clear information, practical tools, resources and an entrepreneurial community that supports real-world judgment that is as important as ever as we harness the power and benefits of AI for business.

For more than 25 years, I've worked with over 25,000 entrepreneurs through business advising, consulting, training and thought leadership, building thousands of business plans while conducting hundreds of workshops, events and public speaking presentations, professional business column writing, along with hosting weekly radio and television business programs.
